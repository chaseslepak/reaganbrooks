How we hold

Built to own, not to trade.

We are a holding company in the oldest sense of the term: we buy good businesses and keep them. Four commitments describe how we work — and how we do not.

I

We buy businesses and keep them.

We are not a fund, and we raise no money to sell what we buy. Nothing we own is for sale, and no purchase is made with an exit in mind. What comes under our roof is meant to remain there.

II

We operate what we own, close to the work.

Our companies are run by people who have spent their working lives in the trade. We keep close to them, and we would sooner learn a business from its floor than from a summary.

III

We answer to no calendar.

There is no quarter to beat and no date by which we must sell. We measure progress in years, and where it is earned, in decades. To us, patience is not idleness but a position, quietly held.

IV

We stay quiet.

Our companies carry their own names into the world. The firm above them has little need to be seen, and less wish to be. Good work, in our experience, seldom needs announcing.
