How we hold

We are built to own, not to trade.

Reagan Brooks is a holding company. We buy good businesses and hold them for the long run. Four commitments describe how we work.

I

We buy businesses and keep them.

We are not a fund, and we do not raise money to sell what we buy. There is no exit built into the purchase. When a company joins us, it joins us to stay.

II

We operate what we own, close to the work.

Our companies are run by people who know the work firsthand, and we stay close to them. We would rather understand a business from the floor than from a summary.

III

We answer to no calendar.

There is no quarter to beat and no date by which we must sell. We measure progress in years, and where it is warranted, in decades. Patience is a position, not a delay.

IV

We stay quiet.

The operating companies carry their own names into the world. The firm above them does not need to be seen to do its work, and it prefers not to be.
